Claveo

Your complete music practice companion

About

Transform your practice sessions with Claveo—the all-in-one app designed for musicians who demand precision and organization. Whether you're a student, teacher, or professional musician, Claveo brings together everything you need to practice effectively and track your progress.

Claveo combines professional-grade tools with an intuitive interface, giving you everything you need in one place. No ads, no subscriptions—just powerful tools designed for serious musicians.

Features

🎵 Practice Recording

  • Record unlimited practice sessions with crystal-clear audio quality
  • Organize recordings with custom names, tags, and piece associations
  • Adjust playback speed from 0.75x to 1.5x for detailed practice
  • Skip forward or backward 15 seconds with a single tap
  • Search and filter recordings by name, piece, tags, or notes
  • All recordings sync seamlessly across your devices with iCloud

🎼 Advanced Metronome

  • Precise tempo control from 20 to 300 BPM
  • Tap tempo feature—just tap to set your desired speed
  • Customizable beat patterns for complex time signatures
  • Support for all common time signatures (2/4, 3/4, 4/4, 6/8, and custom)
  • Save favorite tempos for quick access
  • Haptic feedback option for silent practice
  • Visual beat indicators with customizable accent patterns

🎯 Precision Tuner

  • Real-time pitch detection with professional accuracy
  • Dual tuning meters: standard view for quick tuning, precise view for fine adjustments
  • Visual feedback with color-coded indicators (green = in tune, orange/red = out of tune)
  • Frequency display option for advanced users
  • Works with any instrument—strings, winds, brass, and more
  • Customizable A4 reference frequency (400-480 Hz)

📚 Music Dictionary

  • Searchable database of 650+ music terms and definitions
  • Curated definitions from Open Music Theory
  • Musical symbols with verified SMuFL notation support
  • Organized by categories: Theory, Tempo, Dynamics, Articulation, Notation, Form, and more
  • Examples and detailed explanations for each term
  • Perfect for students learning music theory and notation

Perfect For

Claveo is designed for musicians at every level:

  • • Music students tracking practice progress
  • • Teachers recording lessons and providing feedback
  • • Professional musicians maintaining practice logs
  • • Anyone learning an instrument who needs reliable practice tools

Privacy & Data

Your privacy is important to us. Claveo does not collect, transmit, or have access to any of your personal data. All recordings and metadata are stored locally on your device and optionally synced to your personal iCloud account. We have no access to your data—it remains completely private and under your control.
