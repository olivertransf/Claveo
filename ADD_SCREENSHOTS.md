# How to Add Screenshots

## Quick Steps

1. **Take screenshots** from your iPhone or Simulator
2. **Save them** with these exact names in the `screenshots/` folder:
   - `recordings.jpg` (or `.png`)
   - `metronome.jpg`
   - `tuner.jpg`
   - `dictionary.jpg`
   - `recording-details.jpg`
   - `settings.jpg`

3. **That's it!** The website will automatically display them.

## From iPhone

1. Take screenshots (Power + Volume Up)
2. AirDrop or email them to yourself
3. Save to your Mac
4. Rename and move to `screenshots/` folder

## From Simulator

1. Take screenshot: `Cmd + S` or Device → Screenshot
2. Screenshots save to Desktop by default
3. Move and rename them to `screenshots/` folder

## Tips

- Use JPG format for smaller file sizes
- Crop to remove status bars if desired
- Keep consistent styling (all dark mode or all light mode)
- Optimize images (aim for <500KB each)

The website will show placeholders until you add the images, then automatically display them once added!
