About

Transform your practice sessions with Claveo—the all-in-one app designed for musicians who demand precision and organization. Whether you're a student, teacher, or professional musician, Claveo brings together everything you need to practice effectively and track your progress.

Features

Practice Recording

  • Record unlimited sessions
  • Adjustable playback speed
  • Skip forward/backward controls
  • Search and filter recordings
  • iCloud sync across devices

Advanced Metronome

  • Precise tempo control (20-300 BPM)
  • Tap tempo feature
  • Customizable beat patterns
  • Multiple time signatures
  • Haptic feedback support

Precision Tuner

  • Real-time pitch detection
  • Dual tuning meters
  • Visual feedback indicators
  • Frequency display option
  • Works with any instrument

Music Dictionary

  • Searchable music terms
  • Musical symbols database
  • SMuFL notation support
  • Organized by categories
  • Examples and explanations
