Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

Introduction

Claveo ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

Claveo collects the following types of information:

  • Audio Recordings: When you record practice sessions, audio files are stored locally on your device and synced to your iCloud account.
  • Metadata: Information you provide about recordings, including names, tags, notes, piece information, and measure numbers.
  • App Settings: Your preferences and settings within the app, such as metronome tempo, tuner settings, and display preferences.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and improve the app's functionality
  • Store your recordings and metadata for your personal use
  • Sync your data across your devices via iCloud

Data Storage and Security

All audio recordings and metadata are stored:

  • Locally on your device
  • In your personal iCloud account (if iCloud is enabled)

We do not have access to your iCloud data. All data remains under your control and is subject to Apple's iCloud privacy and security policies.

Microphone Access

Claveo requires microphone access to:

  • Record audio practice sessions
  • Provide pitch detection for the tuner feature

Microphone access is only used when you actively use these features. We do not record or transmit audio data without your explicit action.

Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or distribute your personal information or recordings to third parties. Your data remains private and is only accessible to you through your device and iCloud account.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your recordings and data at any time
  • Delete recordings and data directly from the app
  • Disable iCloud sync if you prefer local-only storage
  • Revoke microphone permissions through your device settings

Children's Privacy

Claveo is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
