Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

Introduction

Claveo ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your data is handled when you use our mobile application.

Important: We do not collect, transmit, or have access to any of your personal data. All data remains on your device and in your personal iCloud account.

Data Storage

Claveo stores the following types of data locally on your device:

  • Audio Recordings: When you record practice sessions, audio files are stored locally on your device and optionally synced to your personal iCloud account.
  • Metadata: Information you provide about recordings, including names, tags, notes, piece information, and measure numbers.
  • App Settings: Your preferences and settings within the app, such as metronome tempo, tuner settings, and display preferences.

We do not collect this data. It is stored entirely on your device and in your personal iCloud account (if iCloud sync is enabled). We have no access to any of this information.

How Your Data is Used

Your data is used solely by the app to:

  • Provide app functionality (playing recordings, syncing across your devices)
  • Store your recordings and metadata for your personal use
  • Sync your data across your devices via your personal iCloud account

We do not use your data for analytics, advertising, or any other purpose. We cannot access your data.

Data Storage and Security

All audio recordings and metadata are stored:

  • Locally on your device
  • In your personal iCloud account (if iCloud is enabled)

We do not have access to any of your data. All data remains under your control and is subject to Apple's iCloud privacy and security policies. We do not transmit data to any external servers or third parties.

Microphone Access

Claveo requires microphone access to:

  • Record audio practice sessions
  • Provide pitch detection for the tuner feature

Microphone access is only used when you actively use these features. We do not record or transmit audio data without your explicit action.

Analytics and Tracking

Claveo does not use any analytics services, tracking tools, or third-party data collection. We do not track your usage, collect crash reports, or gather any telemetry data.

Data Sharing

We do not collect, share, sell, or distribute your personal information or recordings to anyone. Your data remains private and is only accessible to you through your device and iCloud account. We have no access to your data.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
